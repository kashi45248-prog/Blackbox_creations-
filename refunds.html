Refunds

Our tools are digital downloads, so there is nothing to post back — no return by mail. If something is wrong, the refund goes through the store you bought from.

Bought direct from this site (Polar)

14-day refunds via Polar. Ask for one through the receipt email Polar sent you, or at polar.sh/purchases. Polar may also refund up to 60 days after purchase at its discretion, to prevent chargebacks.

Bought on Gumroad

Refunds follow Gumroad's own policy — request one through your Gumroad receipt or purchase page.

Bought on the Unity Asset Store

Unity's own policy: all Asset Store sales are final, with no refunds except the exceptions in its terms. An automatic refund is available only if you ask within 14 days of purchase and the asset has not been downloaded. Unity may also refund within 2 weeks if the asset was not as advertised, is not compatible with the latest Unity release, or includes unauthorised intellectual property. Otherwise, contact the publisher first — that is us at supportblackboxcreations@gmail.com — and if there is no reply in 14 days, Unity reviews it. Refund requests go through your Asset Store Order History. There is no return by mail: the asset is a download, and a refund means the money back, not sending anything back. Source: Unity Asset Store Terms and EULA.

Something doesn't work?

Email supportblackboxcreations@gmail.com first. Most problems are a wrong Unity version or a missed import step, and those are fixed with support — not a refund.
